# Cost-accounting completeness review — 2026-07-29 (post-v4.70.1)

5-area multi-agent audit (service coverage, storage/transfer, AI/Cortex chain, filter threading,
cross-panel reconciliation), every finding adversarially verified. Both defects AND the verified
coverage inventory per cost surface.

## Executive summary

The core credits-accounting engine is sound: every METERING_DAILY_HISTORY service type lands exactly once via an unfiltered MERGE-keyed loader, the cloud-services adjustment is netted into billed dollars exactly once through a single shared formula, the compute/AI partition is an exact set-complement over one canonical predicate, unknown service types degrade visibly rather than vanish, storage uses the correct calendar-month-average binary-TiB basis, the org rate-card is consistently honored as billing truth, and estimated/verified savings never mix — no cost surface is dropped or double-counted at the account-total grain. The one systemic dollar defect is rate application, not capture: AI/Cortex credits are dollarized at the $3.68 compute rate in every all-service rollup (MTD, pace, forecasts, contract pacing, renewal planner, compare, Brief, and the budget-pace/forecast alerts), overstating those headline dollars by (compute − AI rate) × AI credits and breaking tie-out against the Spend page that prices the same rows correctly — the app's registry itself declares the contract these surfaces violate, and the fix pattern (fact_daily_spend_compute) is already proven in the codebase. Below that sit a band of bounded mediums — QAS dropped from proc/pipeline measured costs, fail-safe-only databases and loader-stall gaps silently understating storage, missing coverage guards on contract consumed, undisclosed compute-only scope on the chargeback export and contract-burn surfaces, an AI-fact boundary-day clobber, absent AI alerting, and four company-attribution divergences — and a long tail of low-severity filter/window/label honesty items, almost all cheap app-layer fixes with only three requiring a migration (the alert-proc re-derivation and the AI loader window). Overall: the accounting foundation deserves trust; the AI rate split is the single highest-leverage fix, after which the remaining work is disclosure hygiene and edge-case guards rather than structural repair.

## Findings (severity-ranked; APP = python fix, MIG = migration)

### C1. [HIGH · CONFIRMED · APP] AI/Cortex credits priced at the compute rate ($3.68) in every all-service billed rollup (MTD, pace, forecast, year projection, renewal planner, compare, Brief, budget-pace/forecast alerts)
- **Surface:** All-service billed spend (AI/Cortex credits inside FACT_METERING_DAILY rollups) · `app/ui/pages/overview.py:85`
- mart_sql.fact_daily_spend/fact_daily_spend_year, health_strip's MTD arm, mart27_sql.compare_billed, and the V060 SP_ALERT_SCAN MTD CTEs all sum CREDITS_BILLED across ALL service types with no AI split, and every consumer multiplies the mixed total by the single compute CREDIT_PRICE_USD: overview.py:85-86/:113/:178/:198-200/:379, brief.py:61-67, contract.py:109-124 and :348-353, compare.py:121-122, and V060:1017-1020/:1064-1067 (COST_BUDGET_PACE, COST_FORECAST_BREACH). Meanwhile spend.py:85-88 and ai_chargeback.py price the identical fact rows at AI_CREDIT_PRICE_USD ($2.20 default), and metric_registry.py:86-90 declares AI spend 'Priced at the configured AI rate, not the compute rate.' Two surfaces both labeled billed spend therefore disagree by (3.68-2.20) x AI credits; exec KPIs, forecasts, contract pacing, the renewal-planner commit, and two seeded alerts overstate the bill; and the Spend page's 'why totals differ' expander (spend.py:102-120) never names this rate axis. Only the rate-card reconciliation leg was fixed (triage #6, fact_daily_spend_compute) — every other consumer still mixes.
- **Fix:** Split AI from non-AI in the shared readers: extend fact_daily_spend/fact_daily_spend_year, health_strip's MTD arm, contract_exhaustion, and compare_billed to emit CREDITS_BILLED_AI (SERVICE_TYPE ILIKE 'AI%' OR '%CORTEX%' OR '%INTELLIGENCE%' — the exact predicate already proven in fact_cortex_daily_spend/fact_daily_spend_compute) and CREDITS_BILLED_OTHER, then dollarize as other*rate + ai*ai_rate in overview.py, brief.py, contract.py, and compare.py (ideally via one blended helper in formulas.py per the credits-to-USD-only-in-formulas law). Queue the alert leg for the next proc re-derivation (V061): read AI_CREDIT_PRICE_USD from SETTINGS alongside :credit_price and build MTD_USD as the two-partition sum. Until the split lands, add the rate-mix axis to the 'why totals differ' expander and the affected KPI helps.

### C4. [MEDIUM · CONFIRMED · APP] Storage MTD silently understates when the daily storage loader stalls — no coverage guard, live fallback fires only on empty

### C5. [MEDIUM · CONFIRMED · MIG] FACT_AI_USAGE_DAILY daily loader clobbers the boundary day: CURRENT_TIMESTAMP window overwrites complete day rows with a partial recount

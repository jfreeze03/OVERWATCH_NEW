## Conventions — read this first

These cross-cutting rules answer most "what does this mean / why this number" questions.

**Money & credits.** Snowflake bills **credits**; the app prices them in dollars.
- **Compute rate** = `CREDIT_PRICE_USD` (default **$3.68/credit**); **AI/Cortex rate** =
  `AI_CREDIT_PRICE_USD` (default **$2.20/credit**). Both are editable on **Admin ▸ Settings**
  (the code constants are offline fallbacks only).
- `$ = credits × rate`. AI/Cortex credits are priced at the AI rate; everything else at the
  compute rate; a **blended** figure prices each stream at its own rate.
- Dollars are always run through `format_usd`; a table's `_USD` columns get a `$`.

**Measured vs allocated $ (important for query/pattern costs).**
- **Measured** = exact `QUERY_ATTRIBUTION_HISTORY` credits for a query, **warehouse idle
  excluded** — the true cost of that query. Lags ~8h.
- **Allocated** = the query's execution-second **share** of the whole warehouse-hour bill,
  **idle included** — "who owns the bill". Reads higher than measured. The two are shown
  side by side deliberately (they answer different questions), not by mistake.

**Mart vs live.** A **mart** (`MART_*`, `FACT_*`) is a pre-aggregated table refreshed by a
scheduled task — cheap to read, may lag its cadence. A **live** read hits
`SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE.*` directly — current but expensive and itself lagged by Snowflake
(~45 min, metering up to 24h). Most panels are **mart-first with a labeled live fallback**.

**Time: account vs server.** Under Streamlit-in-Snowflake, SQL `CURRENT_DATE()` /
`CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()` are **UTC** (the account TZ is a no-op), while the app's `account_now()`
/ `account_today()` are **account-local** (America/Chicago). "Yesterday", "complete days",
and the exhaustion clock use account time. A per-day rate divides by the days actually
elapsed in the window.

**Percentiles.** **p95** (e.g. "95s") = the value **95%** of measurements fall under —
the near-worst case a normal user feels, not the average. **p50** = the median. On the
mart path p95 may be the **peak daily** p95 (one bad day penalizes), labeled where shown.

**Humanization.** Bytes → `humanize_gb`/`humanize_bytes` (binary MB/GB/TB); durations →
`humanize_duration` (e.g. `95s`, `2h 3m`); a `%` column shows the `%` sign. A **dash (—)**
means telemetry was unavailable, **not zero**.

**Company scope.** The account is shared (ALFA + Trexis). Most panels honor the **Company**
filter by warehouse/database/role ownership; account-level events ride along as `ALL`.
A per-viewer **watchlist** spans companies.

**Anomaly / robust-z.** Spikes use a **median/MAD modified z-score** (not mean/std, which a
spike inflates to hide itself). A flag needs `|z| ≥ 3.5` **and** materiality (≥ $50 on the
day, ≥ 10 active days) so an idle warehouse's trivial day doesn't cry wolf.
